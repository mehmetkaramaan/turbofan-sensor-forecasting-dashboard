How to read this visualization

  1. At cycle t, only measurements observed up to that cycle are shown.
  2. The model uses the preceding 30 cycles to predict temperature at t+1. The star marks the next, unrevealed target forecast.
  3. Advancing one cycle reveals the measurement and updates the residual and cumulative metrics.

Scientific scope and limitations

This visualization replays FD001 measurements and computed model outputs. It does not solve internal temperature fields, thrust, or combustion physics. Combustor color is an illustrative accent; the T50 indicator represents the LPT outlet measurement.
